# Curated lexical-review register

The Wordnik game wordlist does not label parts of speech, capitalization,
proper names, abbreviations, or senses. This register records entries that are
easy to misclassify and must remain visible to a reviewer.

| Word | Decision | Rationale |
| --- | --- | --- |
| FLAKE / FLAKES / FLAKING | retain | Established ordinary word and ordinary inflections. |
| OHO | retain | Established interjection; rarity is not an exclusion criterion. [Collins](https://www.collinsdictionary.com/dictionary/english/oho) |
| QI | retain | Established ordinary noun in English word-game dictionaries. [Merriam-Webster](https://www.merriam-webster.com/dictionary/qi) |
| ZA | retain | Established informal noun; not rejected merely for being short. [Merriam-Webster](https://www.merriam-webster.com/dictionary/za) |
| JO | retain | Has ordinary-word uses; a personal-name use alone is insufficient to reject it. [Collins](https://www.collinsdictionary.com/dictionary/english/jo) |
| FBI / NASA | reject | Initialisms without an independently established ordinary-word sense under this policy. |
| AARON / OHIO | reject | Proper-name/geographical-name examples for this package policy. |

Unresolved classifications are never inferred from rarity or source spelling.
Add proposed entries and evidence here before moving them to an allowlist or
denylist.
